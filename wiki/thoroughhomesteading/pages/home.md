Thorough Homesteading adds the Hearth, a block that brings villagers to a world that has no village. No villager appears from nothing. You build a home with a free bed and a free workstation, give the Hearth food, and a settler walks in. The settler is a vanilla villager, so every other mod's villager features keep working.

## The idea

1. Place a Hearth and light it with flint and steel or a fire charge.
2. Build a bed and a workstation within the Hearth's radius, with enough light at the bed.
3. Put food in the Hearth by hand or with a hopper.
4. While all of that holds, a countdown runs. When it ends, the food is spent and a settler arrives.
5. The workstation decides the profession. The nearest free workstation wins.
